# Mission 12: Maren's End (the Dry Night, 1890)

The night the sea went out, told by the ways the raid never went. Players cross the drained bed from the foot of Skerry Light to the Swallow among wrecks and runnels, drive Maren off its rim with bells, go down the lodged wreck into an old Spanish drain, leave the lantern at the cistern grate, and face her at the foot of the fort's sea wall as the tide comes back.

### Character voice notes

- **Ruth Carrow**: a Wrackhaven boatwoman in her thirties who leads the party down. Low, steady and practical, with a soft Cornish-coast burr. She sounds like someone who has pulled bodies out of the sea before and won't panic now.
- **Maren**: Maren Holm, the keeper's wife, drowned in 1887. Her voice is a woman's in her thirties, slowed, hollowed and wet with an underwater echo. She is sad and reproachful rather than monstrous, and at the very end almost tender.
- **Holm** (existing speaker): the old keeper, grief held in check, few words.
