# Mission 14, The Drowned Reach: sound notes

The campaign's finale. The sea has come back with every nation and every year that ever sailed through the skerries, and they are at war over the dark Light. You land on the Ember Isles' holm, cross the drying shoal behind Plimsoll, take the station's rock from the Ember Commodore, carry the hoarded oil up, and light it, then keep it lit against the fire-ships. The epilogue is the last page of Holm's log. The mood climbs from grey dusk warfare to night dread to a big, warm lighting of the lamp, then settles into something quiet and bittersweet. The last minute should be the softest thing in the campaign.

Code: `src/cpm14.js` (shared systems), `src/cpm14x.js` (the holm and the shoal), `src/cpm14y.js` (the rock and the lantern), `src/cpm14z.js` (cinematics).

## Voice lines

New speakers:
- **The Wren's Mate** (`rchmate`). Your guide through the Reach: a seaman in his forties off the Wren, steady, wry, long past being frightened by anything but the dark. Measured rather than loud, warm under it; he has known Plimsoll "in any year". His voice should carry the mission; let it soften at the beacons dipping.
- **An Ember Isles Captain** (`rchember`). A soldier of the Ember Isles: hard, clipped, a burr not quite of any real place, a drill-yard bark. Heard mostly off, shouting.
- **The Ember Commodore** (`rchcomm`). The Ember Isles' commander, holding the Light's rock and calling himself its Keeper. Fifties, aristocratic, cold and proud, never hurried until the end; his last line goes almost to a laugh, beaten.
- **An Ice-folk Sergeant** (`rchice`). A northern soldier on the hulk: flat, hard vowels, drill orders shouted in a strange cadence.
- **The Lampwright** (`lamp`). Keep the campaign's existing character: old, precise, a craftsman who ground the lens. Here he is calm and fond, and this is his last word on what a Keeper is.
- **The Keeper's Log** (`log`). The campaign narrator (Holm): measured, tired. The epilogue lines (91 to 95) are the last the players hear: slower, closer, quieter than anything before.
