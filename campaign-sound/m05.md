New speakers:
- **The Wren's Master** (`wren`): master of the schooner Wren, a coasting merchantman. A man in his fifties, weathered, West Country accent, dry and unshowy; never raises his voice unless he must, and then it carries the length of the ship.
- **A Hand off the Wren** (`hand`): a young deckhand of about nineteen, local, quick-tongued, frightened and trying not to show it; his jokes are how he copes.
