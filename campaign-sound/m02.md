### Character voice notes

- **Sergeant Ybarra**: a Spanish garrison sergeant in his forties. Rough-edged and loyal, he gives short orders and speaks English with a light Castilian accent. He sounds like a man who has done this for years and hates it.
- **The Governor**: the fort's governor, in his sixties. Cultured, cold and unhurried, he never raises his voice. He speaks English with a Castilian accent.
- **Black Jago (Jago Penhallow)**: the wreckers' leader, in his thirties. He is a big Cornish-coast man with a rough West Country burr, scornful and proud. He comes back as the Mooncusser, keeping the same voice but slowed and hollowed, with an underwater echo.
- **A Lookout**: a young English sailor at the masthead, heard from far off.
