# m18 · Slack Water (Act V, the years after; added later, after The Drowned Reach): sound notes

The mission: Skerry Clash's own tale. A second light is lit on the rock across the tidal race called the Race, and its keeper swears the old one is the false light. Stage one, **The Second Light**: dusk on the near rock, the Lamp Towers trimmed with oil cans; the far rock kindles a light of its own (cutscene), the tide falls, and the first slack water is three minutes of his hands (the drowned, shore crabs, his gulls, the Drowned Sailor, the Diver) coming over the causeways for your lights. The tide turns (cutscene, and a card: **every slack water after**). Stage two, **Slack Water**: the next slack water (cutscene); cross and fell his two Lamp Towers before the tide turns, his towers shooting down red aim lines, powder kegs off the lamp oil, the Mooncusser crossing late; level at the turn means one minute more. He calls his champion (cutscene: Plimsoll out of the surf). Stage three, **The Champion**: the tide turning, Plimsoll lunging at your towers, his Great Light to put out before the causeways go under; worn down, Plimsoll stops on the causeway, looks from one light to the other, and walks into the sea (cutscene). Outro: the tide back over the causeways, the far keeper promising to relight, Maren with her lantern on the skerry between the two lights.

## Character voice notes

- **The Keeper of the Near Rock** (new): the player's keeper, a man in his forties in an oilskin and keeper's cap, proud of a light he has kept for years and never thought to defend. A Northumbrian coast voice, plain and dry, quick to indignation, brisk under fire. Open air, the Race roaring behind him; no reverb.
- **The Keeper Across the Race** (new): the rival, younger, louder, sure of himself and enjoying every minute; a West Country or Cornish lilt so the two can be told apart at once. Nearly all his lines are shouted across a hundred yards of fast water: roll off the low end, add a little distance and slap from the rocks. Uses the Race's customary shouts (Ahoy!, Well played, Nooo…) without irony.
- **A Grey Seal** (new): no voice; a real grey seal's bark and snort (the subtitle is a stage direction).
- **Maren**: as elsewhere in the Chronicle (warm, low, unhurried, a lantern in her hand); here very close and quiet, out in the middle of the Race between the two lights.
- **The Keeper’s Log**: narration as in the other missions, read back years later by lamplight.
