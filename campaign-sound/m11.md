## Character voice notes

- **The Mate** (new, cast `mate`): the keeper's cutter's mate, forties, an experienced Northumbrian seaman. Brisk, practical, dry, steady under pressure; he shouts a lot (over wind, sea and the fight), so record those lines shouted, not just loud.
- **Holm** (`holm`, shared): the keeper of Skerry Light in 1889. Fifties, clipped, grim, Scots-tinged.
- **The Fog-Keeper** (`fog`, shared): the last fire-keeper of Lantern Rock (mission 1's `lastfire`), drowned in the fog he kept. A deep, slow, hollow voice with heavy reverb, slightly pitched down, always a little too close. As the old fire is lit (`cp_m11_32`) the reverb thins and the man shows through, and his last word (`cp_m11_38`, “Kept.”) is an old man's plain voice, like mission 1's last fire-keeper.
- **The Keeper's Log** (`log`, shared): narration.
