# Mission 10: Freebooters' Sea (Spring 1889)

The first crew out of Saltcote with a Gilt & Gull hoard chart: north past Beacon Knoll to Crowsnest Rock, the hoarder's three sights on the island, a dig, a Mooncusser in the pit, and home past the Black Sloops to the factor's table.

### Character voice notes

- **The Mate**: the cutter's mate, a weathered Saltcote man in his forties with a soft Northumbrian burr. Unflappable and dry, he keeps the crew steady with short practical orders and the odd sour joke.
- **The Gilt & Gull Clerk**: a chart-seller for Gilt & Gull, in his thirties. A smooth, eager Bristol clerk with a salesman's patter, always a little too sure of his charts.
- **The Factor**: Saltcote's factor, in his fifties. Dry, exact and unimpressed; he weighs honest and says little.
