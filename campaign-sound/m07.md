### Character voice notes

- **Mr Tregear, the Receiver**: the Receiver of Wreck for the port, in his fifties. He is a Cornish gentleman official, careful and precise, and speaks in a low voice with a soft West Country lilt. He calls his boat crew "gentlemen" and never swears.
- **Black Nell**: the wrecker gangs' leader on the quay, in her forties. She has a hard harbour voice with a strong West Country accent, cool and amused until she is crossed, and then savage.
- **A Wrecker** (the shared `wreck` speaker): one of Kittow's men, a rough coastal voice, shouting.
