## Character voice notes

- **The Fire-Keeper** (cast `fire`, shared with the campaign): head of the fire-keeping family, fifties, broad-chested, weathered; low, gravelly, few words. An old northern coastal accent (Northumbrian/Scots edge), unhurried even in danger.
- **Wenna** (new, cast `wenna`): his daughter, about seventeen. Quick, clear, practical, braver than she sounds; the same accent, lighter.
- **The Shipmaster** (new, cast `m01mast`): master of a cog lost in the fog; heard only once, shouting from his deck far off. West Country, hoarse, frightened. Heavy distance/echo.
- **The Last Fire-Keeper** (new, cast `lastfire`): the family's last keeper in 1841, an old man in oilskins. Soft, dry, stubborn; a little bitter humour. He becomes the Fog-Keeper: keep a faint likeness to that voice (mission 11's line `cp_m11_38`, “Kept.”, should sound like him).
- **The Keeper's Log** (`log`, shared): narration.
