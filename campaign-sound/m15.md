# m15 · The Nine Holes (Act II, side mission, c. 1883): sound notes

The mission: the still summer evening the keepers lay out their nine holes of golf along the headland above the harbour, and play the round on into the night. Stage one, **The Laying Out**: the inland holes in the afternoon sun (the 1st to the 3rd), one ball between the keeper Holm, his wife Maren, the assistant keeper Pellow and the players; on the 3rd, men stand up in the cove's shallows and come up the shingle. Cutscene: **the sunset** (the 4th to the 6th go by; from the 6th the sun goes down, the Light stays dark, and Pellow, the duty keeper, runs for his skiff). Stage two, **The Chasm**: dusk into moonlight on the 7th and the 8th; the 8th's second shot carries over a sea-filled gully where the summer's lost balls lie shining, and the drowned climb out of it, two of them taking the ball and running for the edge with it. Cutscene: **the ship** (a brig standing in for the point with the Light still dark). Stage three, **Homeward Cliff**: night on the 9th; burning balls struck from a tar pot onto three barrels on the wreckers' ledges under the east cliff to warn the brig off, then the **Chasm Crab** (a Keelback of the gully's old wrecks with the eleven lost balls in its shell) up out of the chasm. Outro: the Light lit at last, the brig safe, the last hole played by moonlight, and the coal book's twelve golf balls.

## Character voice notes

- **Holm** (existing): here in his early forties, some years before the Gannet, Maren alive beside him. The same weathered Northumbrian or Scots-Borders coast voice, dry and low, but lighter, with humour in it until the evening turns.
- **Maren** (existing; his wife, heard alive here, as in m04): about thirty, bright, quick, teasing with Holm and with Pellow. The same accent as Holm, softer. A boatwoman who reads a ship at a glance.
- **Pellow** (new): the assistant keeper, in his early twenties, eager, a little vain about his golf, mortified at missing his lighting time. A Cornish or West Country lilt (keepers were posted far from home), quick and boyish.
- **The Brig’s Master** (new, one line): a gruff merchant master in his fifties bellowing orders through a speaking trumpet, heard far off across the water: thin, distant, torn by the wind.
- **The Keeper’s Log**: narration, as in the other missions; here a fond memory rather than a grim record.
