# Mission 6, The Kittiwake: sound notes

28 February 1888. A night aboard the Kittiwake (WH 31), a Wrackhaven drifter, told the way her one surviving hand told it to the keeper. The mood is close, cold and frightened: a small wooden boat in the dark, a few lamps, and a smiling thing that wants to help. Keep the music sparse so the creaks, the bells and Verity's voice carry.

Code: `src/cpm06.js` (the ship, flares, Verity's rules) and `src/cpm06x.js` (the three parts and the cinematics).

## Voice lines

New speakers:
- **Skipper Abel Penhallow** (`skip`), master of the Kittiwake. A Cornishman in his fifties, weathered and stubborn, the voice of a man who has fished these grounds for thirty years and won't be told. Gruff and clipped, never shouts unless the boat's at risk. Near the end he goes soft and dreamy as Verity takes him.
- **Jory Tregear** (`jory`), the deckhand who lived to tell it. Nineteen or twenty, Cornish, quick and frightened, brave when it counts. He narrates nothing; he's in it. His last lines in the punt are hushed and shaking.
- **Verity** (`verity`). Keep the existing character (`assets/verity.mp3`): a small, sweet, too-friendly voice, close-miked and slightly too near, with a faint wrongness: pitch it a touch high and add a very short, dry doubled echo. Never menacing in tone; the menace is that he's kind.
- **The Keeper's Log** (`log`): the campaign's narrator (Holm), measured and tired, reading from his own page.
