New speaker:
- **The Coxswain** (`m8cox`): the Light's boatman and coxswain of the keeper's cutter, a Wrackhaven man in his forties, broad local accent, gruff and dependable; frightened tonight but steady with it. In Part III he speaks from the cutter alongside, so his lines there want a little distance and wind.
