# m16 · The Harbour Pool (Act II, c. 1885): sound notes

The mission: a summer evening in the unfinished basin under the cliffs, where the harbour crews play Skerry League with the great striped buoy. Stage one, **The Match**: the keeper's cutter (Blue) against Skipper Dunn's brig the Patience (Orange). Shove the buoy into Orange's goal, with the Gale in a bottle (a cask aft) and the yellow gale buoys to fill it. The crowd is on the walls and the two little lighthouses flash behind the goals. Fog stands off the harbour mouth. Cutscene: **the turn**. Maren runs down the wall: the Hound's buoy has been cut away by Kittow's wreckers, and the packet Sorrel is due in the fog. Stage two, **Out through the Gap**: the boom is hauled aside, and the buoy goes out through the gap and north to the Hound's mooring in thick fog, while Kittow's gigs try to put a line on it and board. Cutscene: **the Rook**. Stage three, **The Rook**: Kittow's black lugger has the buoy in tow. Ram her and break her (her muskets, her grapnels), then fight Kittow on your own deck and take him alive. Outro: the Sorrel goes past the Hound safely, and the next evening's match.

## Character voice notes

- **Skipper Dunn** (new; master of the brig Patience, Orange's captain): a big, red-faced harbour skipper in his forties, a booming Devon or Cornish voice that carries across water. Loves an audience, a born showman, a generous loser who would never admit it. When the wreckers come, the bluster drops at once and he is steady and useful. Open air, a little distance on his lines from the Patience.
- **Kittow** (new; the wrecker chief, whose gang Black Nell later runs): a lean man in his fifties with a low, unhurried, hard Cornish coast voice. Never shouts when a sneer will do, until the end. Out of the fog his lines want a little damp distance; on the cutter's deck, close and breathless.
- **Holm** and **Maren** as in the other missions (1885: Holm younger, Maren alive and lively, calling from the wall).
- **The Keeper's Log**: as in the other missions; here a little drier, amused by the game.
