# m09 · Keeper's Last Light (Act IV, Winter 1888 to 1889): sound notes

The mission: after the Gannet rose, the drowned came up out of the sea for the keeper's station itself, night after night. Three of those nights. Stage one, **The First Night**: land on the jetty at dusk, board the courtyard's windows before dark, then three waves (the last brings the first Diver up onto the T-head) with the keeper Holm to keep alive in his own yard. Cards: **night after night**. Stage two, **The Ninth Night**: the oil boat has not come; casks carried from the Oil Store, the Boathouse and the courtyard up the tower's stair to the lamp while a crab tide pours in below. Cutscene: **the false light** on the rocks. Stage three, **The Worst Night**: a winter storm, the lantern room under siege, the drowned over the gallery rail, the Mooncusser's false light drinking the Light's; once he is down, the lamp let burn full until dawn. Outro: the drowned walk back down the jetty into the sea, and Holm on his gallery.

## Character voice notes

- **Holm** (the keeper): in his fifties, the man who has kept Skerry Light since before the Gannet, a widower since 1887 (his wife Maren lost that winter). Weathered, plain-spoken, a Northumbrian or Scots-Borders coast voice, low and dry; says "she" and "her" of the lamp. Never panics in words, though his breath does; fierce when the lamp is threatened, tender only to the lamp. On the lantern floor, add a little glass-room reverb; in stage one, open air and surf. His last line (cp_m09_49) is the quietest, and the closest he comes to a smile.
- **The Keeper's Log**: narration, Holm's own written voice read aloud, slower and flatter than his speech, as if read back years later by lamplight. A faint room tone, no effects.
