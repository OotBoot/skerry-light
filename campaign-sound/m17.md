# m17 · The Reef Board (Act II, c. 1886): sound notes

The mission: a long winter night at Skerry Light, from Skerry Chess. Holm and his assistant keeper, Silas Venn, are at the reef board Holm carved (four rocks, the gold Light squares between) in the Keeper's Study under the lantern. Stage one, **The Middle Watch**: a gale and sleet; the lamp smokes and must be trimmed up in the lantern room, and from the gallery three Wreckers' lanterns are counted out on the skerries for the log; then sit down to the board. Cutscene: **the dream**, Holm asleep over his move. Stage two, **The Gold Squares**: the reef board as he dreamt it, the size of a harbour, granite ledges in black water; each player takes up a carved piece (gannet, cutter, tower, lantern) and is carried square to square by its rules, while the Wreckers' pieces (gang men with lanterns and pistols) move on the beat of the Light's beam. Light the four gold squares. Stage three, **The Wreckers' Keeper**: their keeper rises with a false light on a pole that darkens the gold and runs the real lamp down; Holm walks the board as the Light's keeper. Shoot out the false light and see Holm onto the gold. Outro: a keeper on the Light, and Silas shaking Holm awake an hour before dawn, gold paint on his hands.

## Character voice notes

- **Holm** (the keeper): in his forties in 1886, principal keeper of Skerry Light, married to Maren, a year before the Gannet. Weathered, plain-spoken, a Northumbrian or Scots-Borders coast voice, low and dry, with more humour in it than in his later lines; says "she" and "her" of the lamp. In the lamp room, close and warm; on the reef board (stage two and three, the dream), the same voice with a soft, wide reverb, as if it came off the water.
- **Silas Venn** (new; the assistant keeper): in his twenties, a Wrackhaven lad who came to the Light as assistant; quick, cheeky, stubborn at the board, fond of Holm. A lighter, local West Country edge. Calls him "Mr Holm". Every line of his in stages two and three comes from outside the dream: muffled, as if through a wall or a doze.
- **The Wreckers' Keeper** (new): the Wreckers' own keeper piece made flesh in Holm's dream, a big man in a tarred coat with a false light on a pole. Deep, slow, pleased with himself, never shouting; a faint second, lower voice doubled under his, and dream reverb.
- **A Wrecker**: one of the gang, as in the other missions; coarse, bitter, shouted across water.
- **The Keeper's Log**: narration, Holm's own written voice read aloud, slower and flatter than his speech. A faint room tone, no effects.
