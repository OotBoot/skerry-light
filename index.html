    Skerry Light

    In the winter of 1887 the lamp at Skerry Light failed, and the keeper's wife, Maren Holm, rowed out with a lantern to warn the brig Gannet. The Gannet broke up on the rocks. Maren's skiff came back empty.

    She still stands on the water when the lamp turns, a little nearer every time. The only thing she will not go near is Plimsoll, the shark who walked out of the surf that winter in the Gannet cabin boy's blue shoes. He knows the deep water through the skerries. Follow his shoes.

    • See rocks, wrecks and drifting timber show only while the beam is on them, then they are gone
    • Follow Plimsoll's glowing shoes. While he is close, Maren falls back
    • Ring the bell (Space) while you can see her to drive her away. It takes a while to swing again
    • Steer ← → or A / D, sail ↑ ↓ or W / S. On a phone, hold the left or right side; the middle slows you
    • Hull three hits and she goes down. Let Maren reach the bow and she comes aboard
    • Crew sail with up to two friends. Ships within 90 m of each other keep her back, and a bell rung near a crewmate drives her off both ships
