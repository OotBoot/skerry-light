The Original · Skerry Light as it began

Skerry Light

You are the keeper. Ships are standing in for the harbour through the skerries, and they can't see the rocks. Swing the beam to show them.

  • Aim the beam with the mouse, ← → or A / D, or drag a finger
  • The ring round a ship fills while your beam is on her. While it holds any light she remembers every rock you show her; let it run dry and she forgets them all
  • Ships gather speed all night, with no top speed
  • The fog bell (Space, or double tap) slows every ship for a few seconds. It swings again in 18 seconds
  • Lose three ships in a night and you're relieved of duty
